## Why it exists

DOST started as my own clothing brand. Pretty product pages were the easy part.
Selling for real meant stock, identity, shipping quotes, payment, and order
state all had to agree on a single purchase flow. If any of those pieces lied to
the others, someone would pay for something that should not have been sold.

So I built the storefront myself. Product discovery, auth, cart, shipping
quotes, Stripe Checkout, and order management live in one responsive app for
both signed-in customers and guests. I own the brand and the engineering: API
routes, persistence, payments, shipping, cart behavior, and the order paths that
tie them together.

## Choices I care about

I treat current database values as authoritative instead of trusting cart prices
that arrived from the browser. Availability gets checked again at checkout
boundaries, because stock is a moving target and a stale cart should not win.

Payments stay inside Stripe Checkout. I did not want the store collecting
sensitive card details directly. Guests can still look up an order later, but I
do not pretend that guest lookup is the same thing as a full customer account.

## Limits

The purchase path depends on third-party payment and shipping services. External
failures have to be handled without celebrating an incomplete checkout as a
completed order. The product is active. I do not publish customer data, revenue,
conversion, transaction volume, or payment-reliability claims, and the source
repository stays private.
