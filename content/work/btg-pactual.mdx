## What I do there

I work on foreign-exchange software that has to behave correctly under real
operational pressure. My official title is IT Assistant. Day to day I sit with
the full path of a feature: understanding what traders and domain experts
actually need, helping shape how the system should respond, writing code on both
sides of the stack, and making sure the change is ready for production.

I started as a Software Engineer Intern in May 2023 and moved to IT Assistant in
May 2025. Software Engineer is still how I describe my craft. IT Assistant is
the official title of this role, and I keep that distinction deliberate.

## How the work feels

Most interesting problems here are not “write a screen” or “write an endpoint”
in isolation. They sit between product rules, integrations, persistence, and
validation. I spend a lot of time translating messy business behavior into
something the system can enforce consistently, then checking that the
implementation still makes sense with the people who own the domain.

That usually means collaborating closely with traders, domain experts, and other
engineers. We argue about expected behavior, find the constraints early, and
only then push the change through the rest of delivery.

## A few things I am proud of shipping

I have taken an external integration and an FX workflow all the way through
under correctness, reliability, and operational constraints. I have also worked
on modernizing backend responsibilities toward clearer service-oriented
boundaries, and on concurrent processing with automated validation in paths
where timing actually matters.

I cannot describe internal topology, counterparties, or metrics here. What I can
say is that the work rewards careful engineering: features have to be
understandable, testable, and safe enough to run where mistakes are expensive.
