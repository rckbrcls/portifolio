## Built to keep me on track

I made Sparky because I needed a calmer place for the things I keep forgetting: ideas, plans, reminders, and the work I actually want to finish. It brings scheduled Memories, notes, checklists, links, photos, and files into one desktop workspace — no account, no hosted backend.

## Four views, one calm workflow

Calendar keeps scheduled Memories visible across day and month views. Mind organizes them into nested areas. Focus runs configurable work and break sessions, while Me summarizes weekly completion activity and rhythm.

## Local by design

Sparky stores its main data with SwiftData and keeps attachment payloads in the Mac's Application Support directory. It does not require an account, custom backend, analytics, or advertising.

A mobile version is coming soon.
