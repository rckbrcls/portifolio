## Built to keep me on track

I made Sparky because I needed a calmer place for the things I keep forgetting:
ideas, plans, reminders, and the work I actually want to finish. It brings
scheduled Memories, notes, checklists, links, photos, and files into one desktop
workspace. No account. No hosted backend.

## Four views, one calm workflow

Calendar keeps scheduled Memories visible across day and month views. Mind
organizes them into nested areas so related thoughts do not dissolve into a
flat list. Focus runs configurable work and break sessions when I need a timer
that stays on my machine. Me summarizes weekly completion activity and rhythm
so I can see whether the week was real or just busy.

## Local by design

Sparky stores its main data with SwiftData and keeps attachment payloads in the
Mac’s Application Support directory. It does not require an account, a custom
backend, analytics, or advertising. The point is to stay useful offline and
boring about privacy.
