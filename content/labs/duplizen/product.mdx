## Built for friends first

It started with an impostor-style game night. I wanted each person to see their role on their own phone, and I could not find something that felt right — so I built Duplizen for us to play together.

Create a room, share the code, and join from any browser. Role assignment, round state, voting, and results stay in sync so the group can focus on the conversation.

## How a round works

1. Create or join a room
2. Reveal your role
3. Discuss the secret word
4. Vote and play again

## Made for quick group play

No native install is required. Duplizen works across desktop and mobile browsers, can be installed as a PWA, and supports English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
