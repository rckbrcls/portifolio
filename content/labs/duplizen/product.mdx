## A party game that runs in the browser

Create a room, share the code, and let each player join from their own phone. Duplizen handles role assignment, round state, voting, and results so the group can focus on the conversation.

## How a round works

1. Create or join a room
2. Reveal your role
3. Discuss the secret word
4. Vote and play again

## Made for quick group play

No native install is required. Duplizen works across desktop and mobile browsers, can be installed as a PWA, and supports English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
