## Built for friends first

It started with an impostor-style game night. I wanted each person to see their
role on their own phone, and I could not find something that felt right, so I
built Duplizen for us to play together.

You create a room, share the code, and join from any browser. Role assignment,
round state, voting, and results stay in sync so the group can focus on the
conversation instead of wrestling with the app.

## How a round feels

A session is simple on purpose. Someone creates or joins a room, everyone
reveals a role on their own device, the group discusses the secret word, and
then you vote and play again. The software should disappear into the night.

## Made for quick group play

No native install is required. Duplizen works across desktop and mobile
browsers, can be installed as a PWA, and supports English, Portuguese, and
Spanish.
