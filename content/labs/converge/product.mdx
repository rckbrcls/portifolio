## Built for a quieter focus loop

Configure work and break cycles, control sessions from the menu bar or compact window, review local history and statistics, and receive system notifications when a phase ends.

## Local by design

Focus preferences, session state, and history stay on the device. Converge does not require an account or a hosted backend.

Available for macOS 26 or later through GitHub Releases.
